## Why GodLikeBots?

If you play Age of Empires Mobile long enough, the same pattern shows up every day.

You log in to train troops. You check whether healing is finished. You gather meat, wood, and stone. You collect VIP rewards. You press alliance helps. You queue research. Then you do it again later, because strategy games love timers and timers do not care if you are sleeping, working, or out with friends.

GodLikeBots is built for that layer of the game: the repetitive account maintenance that keeps progress rolling but eats your attention in small chunks all day.

The goal is not to play your battles for you. It is to keep the base work handled so your account is not sitting idle for six hours because one queue finished while you were busy.

## Key Features

### Automated Troop Training

Troop queues are one of those things that look small until you count how many times you reopen the game for them.

The bot can start troop training when resources are available and queues are ready. That keeps your army growing during the dead time between active sessions, which matters more as your account gets larger and training cycles stretch out.

Use it when your aim is simple: more troops tomorrow than you had today.

### Healing After Battle

After fights, injured troops can sit around doing nothing unless you check hospitals and start healing.

GodLikeBots can monitor healing tasks and start recovery when needed. That is a practical win for anyone who joins rallies, fights around objectives, or keeps getting clipped during messy server hours.

A strong account is not only about how many troops you train. It is also about how fast you get wounded units back into the rotation.

### Resource Gathering for Food, Wood, and Stone

Food, wood, and stone are the quiet bottlenecks behind most upgrades.

The tool can automate gathering tasks for these core resources. That means fewer empty march slots, fewer forgotten runs, and less pressure to burn packs just because your base needs one more upgrade before reset.

> **Good fit:** players who run farm accounts or want their main account collecting while they are offline.  
> **Poor fit:** players expecting the bot to make combat decisions in live PvP.

### VIP Reward Collection

Daily VIP rewards are easy to miss because they feel tiny in the moment.

They are not tiny over time.

The bot can collect VIP rewards on schedule, which is useful if you do not check the game at the same hour every day. You avoid the annoying "I knew I forgot something" moment and keep the small freebies stacking.

### Alliance Helps

Alliance helps are low-effort when you press them once.

They become a chore when you are doing it across accounts, resets, and long sessions.

GodLikeBots can press alliance helps automatically so your alliance gets faster build, research, and healing support without you sitting there tapping the same button. It is a small feature with a nice social effect, because active helps make an alliance feel alive.

### Research and Building Upgrades

Research and building timers are where momentum gets lost.

The tool can help with routine research and building upgrades so your account keeps progressing between active play windows. You still set the direction. The bot handles the repeat work that follows.

Think of it as queue discipline, without needing to be glued to the screen.

### Custom Delay Between Cycles

Not every setup should run at the same pace.

Custom cycle delays let you tune how often the bot checks tasks. That matters if you are running an emulator on a weaker PC, using multiple accounts, or trying to keep the workflow stable instead of frantic.

If your emulator starts lagging, widen the delay. If everything runs clean, tighten it a bit.

Simple, but it saves headaches.

### Discord-Based Support

Bot setup is half software and half environment.

Emulator version, window settings, account setup, game patches, and PC resources can all change how smooth the first run feels. GodLikeBots pushes users toward Discord for guides, updates, patch notes, and direct help from the developer.

That is the right place to go when a game update moves a menu or an emulator setting needs a tweak.

## System Requirements

### Minimum Requirements

- **OS:** Windows PC
- **RAM:** 8GB minimum
- **Emulator:** LDPlayer, Nox, or BlueStacks
- **Platform:** PC only for the current setup
- **Account:** Active GodLikeBots membership
- **Setup:** Emulator must be installed and configured before running the bot

### Recommended

- **RAM:** More than 8GB if you plan to run several emulator instances
- **CPU:** Modern multi-core processor for smoother emulator performance
- **Storage:** Enough free space for emulator data and game files
- **Internet:** Stable connection for long farming sessions
- **Discord Access:** Strongly recommended for setup guides and patch updates

> **Practical rule:** one account is mostly about getting the emulator configured. Multiple accounts are about PC resources. If your emulator stutters with one game window, do not stack more instances yet.
